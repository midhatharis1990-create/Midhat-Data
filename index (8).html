SpeedAutoCar Business & Performance Analysis

Challenge & Analysis

Transformed unorganized transactional logs across 68 automotive service jobs into an interactive Power BI visual suite to identify hidden revenue drivers and outstanding collection bottlenecks.

Key Findings & Strategic Impact

  • PPF Dominance: Identified Paint Protection Film as the primary margin engine (PKR 2.51M).
  • Collection Risk: Highlighted PKR 1.75M lingering across smaller jobs needing structured follow-up.
  • Growth Opportunities: Uncovered high-margin pricing potential in Ceramic Coating additions.
